The original checklist

This was the old guide's suggested spec shortlist for a 3-4 year phone, not a current minimum or a guarantee that a phone will last that long.

  • 3+ years of software updates
  • 6-8 GB physical RAM
  • UFS 3.1 or newer storage
  • 128-256 GB storage capacity
  • 5,000 mAh or larger battery
  • 5G support
  • IP64 or higher rating

Use this only as a comparison starting point. The original source did not establish these as universal requirements. Check the exact model, remaining support, condition, warranty, return terms and final price on the seller's page. A current, evidence-backed buying guide is still a gap.
