A starting checklist

A suggested spec shortlist for a phone to last 3-4 years, not a current minimum or a guarantee that a phone will last that long.

  • 3+ years of software updates
  • 6-8 GB physical RAM
  • UFS 3.1 or newer storage
  • 128-256 GB storage capacity
  • 5,000 mAh or larger battery
  • 5G support
  • IP64 or higher rating

Use this only as a comparison starting point. Check the exact model, remaining support, condition, warranty, return terms and final price on the seller's page.
