Before the door opens

  1. Match the car and driver.

    Check the registration plate, car make/model and driver photo against the app. Do not get in if they don't match.

  2. Share the trip when you want someone to follow along.

    Use your ride app's trip-sharing feature with a trusted person.

  3. Stay aware of the route.

    Keep track of where you are going.
