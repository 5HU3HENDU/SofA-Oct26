Resigning

Close one chapter carefully. Leave the handover clear.

Before the conversation

  1. Be clear about your choice.

    The original guide asks you to decide whether you want to leave or discuss staying before speaking with your manager or HR.

  2. Check the actual terms.

    Read your offer, employment terms and company policy. Confirm dates, notice and settlement questions directly rather than assuming the old guide applies.

A calm handover

  • Finish or clearly hand over pending work.
  • Document what the next person needs to know.
  • Keep relationships respectful.
  • Keep copies of your own payslips and employment documents you are allowed to retain, without taking confidential company material.

The old claims that notice-period workload will be lighter, unused leave will always be paid, or a fixed notice period must always be served are not repeated as rules. These depend on your contract, employer policy and applicable law. This is an organizing guide, not employment or legal advice. No resignation message is sent here.

Adapted from the original School of Adults Resigning page, checked 11 October 2026. Contract-specific rights, pay and leave questions remain outside this source-only checklist.
