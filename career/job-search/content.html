Job Search

Find leads. Prepare real examples. Keep your next move grounded.

Before an interview

  1. Keep the resume honest.

    Be clear about what you know and what you do not.

  2. Prepare a real example.

    Use STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Explain your responsibility, what you did and what happened.

  3. Practise the explanation.

    Rehearse your projects and the questions you expect. Reach out to your network for relevant leads.
