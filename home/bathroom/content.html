Bathroom

Keep the everyday things easy to reach, then use the right help for the messy jobs.

A place for the basics

  • Use a towel rail or ladder if hooks are not working for your space.
  • Keep a bin with a lid.
  • Give the floor wiper, bucket and mug a place.
  • Keep liquid handwash by the sink.
  • Group shampoo, soap, facewash and razor in a wash bag or organizer.
