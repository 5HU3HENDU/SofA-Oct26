Exercise

The original movement, hobby and equipment resources. Not a prescribed workout.

Walking, running, swimming, yoga, gym, outdoor sports and Zumba were the original activity ideas. Crosswords, reading, drawing, puzzles and doodling were the hobby ideas. Choose activities for your ability and situation.

The old fixed 40-minute stretching timer, neck-roll instructions and blanket stair/commute advice are held. A library, product or article does not establish that an exercise is safe for you. Check suitability and any medical limitations before following a routine.
