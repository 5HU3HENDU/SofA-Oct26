Food, Water & Air

Food, water and air need context. The old one-size-fits-all rules are held.

Water

Original water-quality article ↗Peore · September 2021 purifier-vendor article, not independent clinical guidanceWater, drinks & hydration ↗NHS general guide · Needs vary with activity, health and conditions

The original “4-5 litres every day” rule is not repeated. The NHS gives general fluid guidance, not one fixed amount for everyone. Follow your clinician's advice if you have fluid restrictions.

Food

The original mentioned meal planning, home cooking, fruit/juice and dry fruit. It did not supply a verified diet plan, quantities or suitability checks. Those remain a gap, not universal nutrition advice.

Air

The original blanket mask and purifier suggestions are held. It supplied no air-quality, filtration or mask-selection guide for the actual environment.

The commercial water article's purifier and TDS claims are not endorsed here. This page does not select a purifier, prescribe a diet or replace health guidance.
