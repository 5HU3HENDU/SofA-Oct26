Buying a Car

Keep the original buying questions. Leave the unsupported numbers behind.

Before choosing

  • Compare new and used options, including the actual warranty and condition.
  • List the full purchase costs and running costs for your situation.
  • Compare financing terms, including processing fees.

Before the purchase

  • Arrange an appropriate test drive and inspection.
  • Check that the invoice matches the selected variant.
  • Check the actual vehicle, registration, insurance and required documents.

After the purchase

  • Keep service records and purchase documents.
  • Check registration/ownership steps and insurance requirements with the relevant authority and provider.

The original blanket percentages for depreciation, used-car discount, on-road cost, EMI limits and negotiation, fixed interest/credit-score/fuel-cost figures and universal warranty terms are held. They are not verified budgets or current lending rules.

Also held: full-throttle test-drive instruction, automatic-car licence endorsements, fixed RC-transfer deadline and same-day zero-depreciation cover requirement. Safe driving, registration deadlines and insurance needs depend on the actual situation and current rules. This page is an organizing checklist, not financial, legal or driving advice.
