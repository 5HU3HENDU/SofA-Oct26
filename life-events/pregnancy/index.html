Hospital bag

A packing outline, not a list of hospital admission requirements. Ask your hospital what it supplies and what you need.

Documents to confirm
  • Hospital notes, scan reports and antenatal records
  • Hospital registration and insurance information requested by your hospital
  • Identification and copies only as required by your hospital
  • Emergency contacts
For you
  • Comfortable clothes and nightwear
  • Bras and breast pads, as appropriate
  • Maternity pads
  • Toiletries
For the baby
  • Nappies
  • Vests / sleepsuits
  • Muslin cloths and a blanket
For your birth partner
  • Phone and charger / battery pack
  • Water and snacks
  • Any payment method needed for hospital charges
Read the NHS hospital bag checklist ↗Checked general packing guide · UK source, not Indian hospital policy

Fixed photocopy, clothing and cash quantities are not hospital rules. This page does not give clinical advice or book an appointment.
